## 2. What is actually implemented (verified in `src/`)

**Flight.** Deterministic 120 Hz point-mass-with-orientation model. Real lift/drag/induced-drag physics; four breeds defined by mass, span and wing area rather than fudge factors:

| Breed | Character | Cruise | Best glide | Roll rate | Stamina (full flapping) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Harrier | all-rounder | 23 m/s | 10.3:1 | 1.76 rad/s | 16 s |
| Skimmer | light, twitchy | 21.4 | 8.8:1 | 2.53 | 12 s |
| Galewing | rides the wind forever | 21.3 | 13.8:1 | 1.01 | 20 s |
| Ironback | a falling mountain | 26 | 9.6:1 | 1.16 | 14.5 s |

Verbs: pitch, bank (auto-coordinated), rudder, flap (committed wingbeats), tuck-dive, flare/air-brake with backwinging near ground. Stamina never locks the wings (fades to weak beats). Soft stall protection, stall recovery pull-out, grace windows after takeoff/cliff/catch/respawn, impact stun, ground effect, gust roll, thermals, ridge lift, lee sink, a boundary storm wall.

**The "flight floor" the review asks for already exists.** Landing is forgiving (flared sink under 12 m/s counts), falling riders are caught by the wyvern after 0.8 s, dropping below the cloud floor respawns you 30 m above last safe ground at cruise speed, and landing for 2 s grants "Rested" (+40 % stamina, +50 % regen for 90 s). Islands are rewards, not obstacles.

**Player states.** flying, landed, onFoot, mounting, dismounting, traversing, falling, respawning. Walking, calling the wyvern, mounting. Rider can leap off in flight into freefall.

**Windline (code name Sky Lance).** Bracer-fired magical tether, usable on foot and in freefall only: 65 m cast (95 m charged), fixed-length slack/taut swing, deliberate inward pull, momentum-preserving release, free-air boost on a 1.75 s cooldown, steerable freefall, hold-to-recall the wyvern. Enabled by default in normal play. This is a large finished system the review does not know about.

**Racing.** Seven courses on save-stable ids: Roost Loop, Cascade Run, Archway Thread, Underspan, Needle Slalom (ring courses, difficulty 1–5) and Lantern Dash, The Long Drift (point-to-point sprints that require thermals). Stone start gates, in-order rings (no time penalty, you go back), per-ring splits with delta to your best, ghosts at 30 Hz in IndexedDB keyed to the breed and look, medals derived from a pace model (34/29/24 m/s average airspeed) that an autopilot proves achievable for every breed in tests, 20 s retry window, rolling start from the menu. No AI rivals, no course variants, no online anything.

**Lantern Dash coach.** Optional HUD-only lift coaching (seek, climb, exit, approach, recover, stall, finish). Presentation only; no physics.

**Progression.** Save v1 (`driftwing.save.v1`). 48 feathers, 7 relics (Ember Scale, Old Bull's Antler, Gale Plume, Winner's Ribbon, Howdah Finial, Wind Charts, Keeper's Circlet), landmark discovery, race records. Unlocks are **all cosmetic** (horns, capes, outfits, saddles) gated by feather counts, specific medals, medal tallies and relics. Breeds and races are never gated. Research: three branches (Natural History, Antiquities, Winds), points fund the "Codex" airship build stages 0–5, but only the first site (Shrine of the First Wind) is live and the displayed stage is capped at 1.

**Cosmetics / Stable.** Wyvern: breed, 5 colours, 7 patterns, 6 horns, 5 frills, 5 tails, 4 saddles. Rider: build, skin, 6 hair, 7 outfits (Driftwing adds Travel Drape, the first Air-Nomad look), 8 mix-and-match garment slots, headgear, scarf, cape, dyes. Windkeel harness exists only behind a debug URL.

**World.** One archipelago, "the Drift": 2,900 m radius, 12 hand-placed landmarks plus ~50 scattered islands, 8 thermals (8–12 m/s), day/night (24 real minutes), seeded Markov weather (clear/breezy/overcast/mist/rain; thermals never below ~0.6 strength). Analytic island shapes shared by rendering, collision, landing and walking. Six Ventari ruin sites with inscriptions. Windhaven airship exists only as a stationary debug showcase (72 × 28 m deck, cabin, shelter, four hitch sockets, wind cloth).

**Input.** Controller-first (RT flap, LT flare, LB+RB tuck, bumpers rudder), full keyboard/mouse, remapped odd gamepads, haptics, per-device invert, FOV, look sensitivity, reduced motion, rumble toggle, tips toggle, ghost toggle. Hold-to-flap or tap-per-beat. No user-selectable camera modes, no steering-assist tiers.

**Tools.** Region Workshop editor (12 islands, 80 props, gate + 20 checkpoints, undo/redo, JSON, sandbox playtest at world offset). ~60 Playwright QA scripts. CI runs typecheck, tests, build, smoke and browser checks on every PR.

**Not in code at all:** multiplayer/networking, AI rivals, bonding, combat, capability (non-cosmetic) unlocks, course variants, dialogue (Quintus/Vellum are speaker ids only), four-legged dragons (rig is a two-leg wyvern, 58 bones), the Craghorn, region travel, moving decks.

## 3. Lore and world bible (canon tiers)

**Authority order** (from `ART_DIRECTION.md` and `DECISIONS.md`): Robert's current direction → implemented source → the art master and pack intro (still labelled "proposed; not final game canon").

**Setting (in code).** The Drift: a floating archipelago over a cloud sea. Title tag: "Wings over the Drift — a game about wind, wings and the long way round." Tone is gentle, wry, non-hostile; even Stormbreak's lightning is planned as "visibility and wind, not an enemy."

**Twelve landmarks** (verbatim descriptions in `layout.ts`): Windward Roost (the rookery and home), Gull's Rest, Cascade Isle, Rootfall, The Old Observatory, Highmoor, Glowcap Grove, The Archway Reach, Mistveil Shelves, The Lantern Spire, The Needles, Eye of the Gale.

**The Ventari (in code).** "The wind-riders who built on these islands before anyone now alive… they charted the winds, they kept wyverns, and then they left." Six sites with inscriptions; motifs are counting winds, the Gale, and a westward departure ("She flew west with the last of us. Keep her saddle oiled." / "The last one we followed does not return."). The first inscription is implemented in provisional Ancient Greek, procedural epigraphic capitals, no accents, lunate sigma; English on reading; no decipherment mechanic. The owner may later turn the Greek into the game's own language, so inscription text is replaceable data.

**Characters.** The player rider is unnamed and fully customizable. Quintus: scholar-naturalist, owner of the Codex (a flying library being built in the Roost dock); reserved for later, no dialogue written. Vellum: a female speaker, by inference Quintus's wyvern. Named-in-lore only: the Colossus rider, the keeper of the Lantern Spire, "Old Bull" of Highmoor, the queen whose wyvern would not leave a Needle.

**Creatures.** Wyverns are reptiles (two hind legs, wing-arms). Research species: Skyling, Driftray (circles where there is lift), Sun Gecko, Woolback, Windharp Reed, Dawnbloom, Glowcap. Chosen but unbuilt: the Craghorn, a crimson rock-climbing fifth breed that "a wider audience will call a dragon."

**Air Nomad art direction (proposed).** "An Aegean-inspired civilization that learned to inhabit the sky with dragons." Chalk linen, sky blue, scale slate, aged bronze, limestone (70/20/10). Chiton, chlamys, scale bib, sandals; the banner-lance as a wind-standard; eight role archetypes (Wanderer, Scout, Pathfinder, Aerie Rider, Warden, Drake Tender, Channeler, Elder); limestone arcades, blue plaster, bronze beacons, wind-bridges. Explicitly no pagodas, no knight plate.

**Website storyboard, chapter 01 (owner-approved direction, story still draft).** Five clans (red, green, blue, black, white) sharing one culture with regional accents; one dragon and one wyvern elder per clan on tower-top platforms; dragons are four-legged plus wings, wyverns are not; colour philosophies inspired by Magic's colour pie; frames "Arrival → The Two Elders → An Old Rivalry → Two Ways To Fly → The Start → The Wind Turns → Through Together → Beyond The Circuit"; a Red/Blue perspective choice that rejoins. The phrase "first cataclysm" appears once; whether the elders witnessed it is unresolved. The eight PNGs are not committed.

**Backlog, explicitly not authorized:** flying multiplayer home ship, multiple companions and hitching, aether gates and beacon exploration, banner lance and moving-object grappling, creature befriending and card encounters, selective destruction. Aerial ball sport is "committed long-term" but unbuilt.

**Unresolved in the docs themselves:** Windhaven versus the Codex (two airships, one story?); Air Nomads as the main culture or one faction; the Craghorn's name; the clans' names and emblems; what "the Circuit" is in-world.

## 4. Assessment of the comparative design review

**Verdict: the review is well sourced, honest about evidence limits, and its proposed loop is the right loop. Its main blind spot is that it was written as if Havens were starting from zero.** Much of its vertical slice already exists in Driftwing, so the real planning question is which gaps to close, not whether to build the slice.

### 4.1 Where the review and the existing game already agree

| Review recommendation | Status in Driftwing |
|---|---|
| Generous basic flight, always-usable floor | Done: soft stall, grace windows, catch, respawn, stamina never locks wings |
| One readable resource | Done: stamina, with thermal-accelerated regen |
| Landing improves the loop | Done: Rested buff; roadmap principle "islands are places to rest" |
| Terrain as partner (arches, dives, thermals, lee) | Done in the Drift: 8 thermals, ridge lift, arches, spires, the Eye |
| Racing uses the same movement as exploration | Done: no race-only physics; courses are on landmarks |
| Local records, splits, ghost after replay is stable | Done: splits with deltas, ghost replay, pace-model medals |
| Instant retry | Done: 20 s restart window, rolling start |
| Exploration → discovery with a route clue | Partly: M1 First Lead implemented up to the first site |
| Readable silhouettes per island | Largely: each landmark has a dominant shape and a name |
| Motion/accessibility options early | Partly: reduced motion, FOV, invert, sensitivity; no horizon-stabilization or assist tiers |
| Controller-first with a small verb set | Done, but the verb set is already as large as the review warns against (flap, flare, tuck, rudder, plus Windline cast/pull/boost/recall on foot) |

### 4.2 Gaps the review correctly identifies

1. **Capability progression.** Every unlock is cosmetic. Nothing changes what the dragon can do in old terrain. The review's "few visible capabilities that change a familiar route" is the biggest missing piece.
2. **Bonding.** Nothing. Not even an idle greeting. The art direction calls for "intelligence and trust"; the roadmap lists bonding under "Later."
3. **Course variants.** No reverse, altitude, weather or capability-restricted variants. Seven courses, each one way.
4. **Discovery with consequence.** Feathers and relics are collection for cosmetics. Only one inscription leads anywhere.
5. **Result-screen feedback.** Splits exist, but nothing says "you lost 4 s leaving the thermal early." The Lantern coach is the only per-course coaching.
6. **Compact authored area.** The Drift is big and mostly scattered. The review would start from one cluster.

### 4.3 Where the review conflicts with existing decisions (needs a call)

- **Normalized race loadout vs. four physically different breeds.** The review wants competitive clarity. The project's rule is "every breed medals on every course" and records are keyed to breed. These are compatible if records are per-breed categories, but someone has to decide.
- **"Combat is secondary" vs. "never hostile."** The review's brief assumes some combat; the roadmap and ambient-life plan are explicitly non-hostile. The Falconeer-style "occasional pursuer" is the only combat idea the review itself endorses.
- **A compact slice vs. the Drift that exists.** Throwing away the Drift to build a small Aegean cluster is expensive; keeping it means the "compact" advice becomes "focus one quadrant first."
- **Dragons vs. wyverns.** The review says "weighty dragons." The code is wyverns on a two-leg rig; the storyboard and the sleek-dragon concept are four-legged. This is the single most expensive fork in the road (rig, animator, saddle, every cosmetic).
- **Windline.** The review never mentions rider freefall or tethered swinging. Keeping it, shelving it, or making it a progression unlock is a scope decision.
- **Aegean visual language vs. the current painterly Drift.** The AER-derived art rules (silhouette, contrast reserved for decisions, detail where players slow down) fit the existing toon renderer, but the Drift's dressing is only partly Mediterranean. The art master is still "proposed."

### 4.4 Ideas worth taking from each reference, mapped to what we have

| From | Take | Existing hook |
|---|---|---|
| AER | Landmark silhouette rules; discoveries that end small stories; flight-only discoveries (follow a creature) | Driftray "circles where there is lift" is already written; `discovery.ts` zones |
| Aloft | One authored airship with functional sockets; dragon upgrades change flying, ship upgrades change reach; completion rewards vs timed rewards | Windhaven (four hitch sockets), Codex stages, research branches |
| WoW | Normal/Advanced/Reverse/Challenge variants on the same course; charges rather than a draining meter is a tuning option, not a mandate | `courses.ts` specs are data; medals come from a pace model so variants are cheap |
| GW2 | Dive-and-climb rhythm; teach each maneuver in safe terrain and show why it worked; player-led practice | Lantern coach phases; tips system; Gull's Rest as the safe meadow |
| HTTYD 2 | A discovery that belongs to this player and this dragon (perch, charm, home display); early interesting capability | Stable screen; Roost; relic descriptions |
| Century | Map-based energy choices; braking and burst mastery; personal-best over hours played; eggs/growth as identity | Thermal placement; flare/tuck; Rested |
| Falconeer | Mass through body and energy exchange, not input delay; perch approach generous and cancellable; occasional pursuer | Flight model already separates intent from body motion; backwinging |
| Sky | Legible flight states; one act of care between players with a solo equivalent | Future co-op; the wyvern catch is already "care" from the dragon |

## 5. A first synthesis: the game Havens probably is

Stated as a hypothesis for you to confirm or correct:

> Havens of the Drift is a controller-first, single-player-first flight game about a rider and one intelligent dragon in a sunlit Aegean sky-archipelago. Learning to fly well changes how you explore; what you discover teaches the winds and bonds you to your dragon; races on the same terrain prove it. A finite story about the vanished Ventari ends; mastery continues. Later, a host's airship becomes a shared home for up to four players. There is no combat loop, no survival economy, no live service.

"Havens" reads naturally as the clan settlements (the tower-top havens of the storyboard) scattered across the Drift. If that is the intent, the name already does narrative work the previous titles did not.

## 6. Decision questions

These are the questions that change what gets built. Each has my recommendation; override freely.

### Identity
1. **Mount: wyvern or dragon?** The code, rig, saddle and every cosmetic are wyvern. The storyboard, the sleek-dragon concept and the Meshy plan point at four-legged dragons. Options: (a) wyvern stays the player mount, dragons are elders/NPCs only; (b) both are player mounts, dragons arrive with the new rig; (c) replace with dragons. *Recommendation: (a) for the slice, (b) as the roster plan. Never (c) before the loop is proven.*
2. **Is Havens the same game as Driftwing with a new name and a sharpened loop, or a new game that reuses the engine?** *Recommendation: same game, same saves, same Drift; the "slice" is a focused pass, not a restart.*
3. **What does "Havens" mean in-fiction?** Clan settlements? Safe perches? Both?

### Flight and progression
4. **What are the three to five capability unlocks?** Candidates that fit existing physics: a stronger thermal climb (bond), controlled slow landing on steeper slopes (the Craghorn's 56° rule generalized), a short precision maneuver (snap-roll through a tight gap), a longer Rested window, Windline from the saddle. *Recommendation: pick three, each tied to one bond milestone and one route it changes.*
5. **Do capabilities come from bond, research, or both?** Research already funds the Codex. *Recommendation: bond → dragon capabilities; research → ship/world reach; cosmetics from everything.*
6. **Keep stamina as is, or move to charges like current Skyriding?** *Recommendation: keep stamina; it is tuned, tested and readable.*

### Racing
7. **Per-breed record categories, or one normalized race breed?** *Recommendation: per-breed categories (already how ghosts are keyed), plus capability tier shown on the record.*
8. **Which course variants first?** Reverse, night/weather, "no thermal" challenge, altitude cap. *Recommendation: Reverse and one Challenge on two existing courses.*
9. **Do AI rivals belong in the first release?** The autopilot can fly every course. *Recommendation: one rival ghost-with-personality per course, after variants.*

### Bonding and story
10. **What is the smallest bond expression you want to see first?** Greeting at the Roost, anticipatory lean before a known maneuver, a response to a hard landing, a resting interaction. *Recommendation: greeting + lean, no meters.*
11. **Does the Ventari mystery resolve in the first release?** M1 ends at Highmoor. *Recommendation: a finite three-lead arc that ends; the "went west" question stays open for the airship era.*
12. **Quintus and Vellum: in or out of the first release?**
13. **Are the five clans and ten elders game canon or comic-only for now?** This decides whether the Roost's red/blue banners mean anything.

### Scope
14. **Combat: none, a pursuer, or secondary combat?** *Recommendation: none in the first release; one optional pursuer prototype later.*
15. **Windline: core, optional, or unlocked?** *Recommendation: keep, present it as an exploration tool unlocked after the first landing discovery, never required on a course.*
16. **Multiplayer order:** (a) nothing until the solo loop is proven, (b) an early bare networking feasibility test alongside, (c) co-op in the first release. *Recommendation: (b), with zero co-op content.*
17. **Aerial ball sport:** still committed, or paused until after the shared airship?

### Production
18. **Which repository seeds Havens?** (a) Driftwing main (newest code) with Dragon-Circuit's docs merged in; (b) Dragon-Circuit with Driftwing's PR #14 merged in; (c) Havens as a design-only repo while code stays in Driftwing. *Recommendation: (a), since the merge is mechanical and keeps saves, CI and the rider work.*
19. **World: keep the Drift and focus one cluster (Roost, Gull's Rest, Cascade, Rootfall), or author a new compact Aegean cluster?** *Recommendation: keep the Drift; re-dress the Roost quadrant to the Aegean language first.*
20. **Art pipeline reality check:** Meshy + Blender for the rider is proven opt-in. Is a Meshy dragon/wyvern body in the first release, or does the procedural wyvern ship?

### The Codex-inherited open items you have not yet answered
21. The Craghorn's name, and free from day one or earned.
22. Windhaven and the Codex: one ship or two?
23. Air Nomads: the player culture, one clan, or a starting outfit family?
