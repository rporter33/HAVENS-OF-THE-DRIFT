# Havens and the Drift — meanings (owner statement, 2026-10-15)

Recorded from Robert's own words. This is canon at the top of the authority order; the art master and older documents are subordinate to it.

- **Havens** are the island monasteries, temples and cities.
- **The Drift** is the wind.
- **The Drift** is also what the cataclysm was and left behind: things of old slowly drifting into the ground; islands, buildings and landscapes drifting apart over time, and sometimes suddenly in a cataclysmic event; people drifting away and migrating. "That's why it's a drift."

## How this connects to what exists in code and docs

- The Ventari ruins (six sites, inscriptions about counting winds and a westward departure) are the "things of old drifting into the ground."
- The twelve landmarks of the Drift and the research branch "Winds" already treat wind as the world's structure.
- The storyboard's "first cataclysm" line (unresolved whether the elders witnessed it) now has a mechanism: drifting apart, slow and sudden.
- The storyboard's five clans and tower-top elder platforms are candidates for what a "haven" looks like.
