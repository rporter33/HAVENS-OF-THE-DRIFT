Rider and dragon texture quality budgets

Robert requested desktop/laptop support, including integrated graphics, and authorized exploring 3–4K at High and 2K at Medium on October 10, 2026 (Mountain time). This document separates the implemented C02 rider proof from the corresponding target for future dragon bitmap materials. Existing WebGL 2, soft toon rendering, efficient dyes and flight behavior remain the baseline.

The runtime caps the rider choice to the WebGL renderer's actual maximum texture dimension. The High comparison query is riderTexture=3072 or riderTexture=4096, alongside riderProof=wanderer|aerie. It never overrides Low's 1K or Medium's 2K budget and is not saved. riderMaps=color remains the default; normal enables the optional tangent normal map and off keeps the material comparison. Ordinary rider catalog/defaults remain unchanged.

What higher resolution means here

The supplied M01 image is an illustrative material study, not a UV-ready map pack. The proof's dye-neutral procedural maps reproduce its dry linen weave, quiet slate grain, worn leather and soft bronze. Each size is generated directly from the material functions; it is not an enlarged 1K bitmap. Identical normalized UVs preserve motif scale and normal relief strength. The skin and cloth boundary mask prevents an interpolated atlas lookup from putting linen detail on skin.

One combined atlas supplies four padded material tiles. Their usable widths are 480, 960, 1440 and 1920 pixels at 1K, 2K, 3K and 4K respectively. These are atlas dimensions, not a separate full-resolution map for every garment. Uniform world-space texel density and a bespoke production unwrap remain art tasks; more pixels alone do not improve silhouette or anatomy.

Memory and fallback

Approximate GPU storage for uncompressed RGBA8 with the full mip chain:

Square atlas Color only Color plus optional normal
1K 5.33 MiB 10.67 MiB
2K 21.33 MiB 42.67 MiB
3K 48 MiB 96 MiB
4K 85.33 MiB 170.67 MiB

These estimates exclude geometry, render targets, shadows, CPU pixel arrays, driver overhead and the rest of the game. CPU arrays also remain for WebGL context restoration: 4/16/36/64 MiB per map. Atlas/material caches are shared across rider instances and dyes. Normal pixels are created lazily. Visited resolutions remain cached, so switching through every size retains more than the active row's budget; the 1K–4K color set is approximately 160 MiB on the GPU, or 320 MiB if every normal variant has also been uploaded. No per-dye copy is created.

3K uses about 44% less map memory than 4K. It is the conservative High default after both rendered successfully in the production browser. Low remains the fallback for integrated graphics. Browser evidence used software SwiftShader and does not establish target-device frame rate, safe peak memory or an automatic memory-pressure fallback. Hardware measurements and a useful image difference at gameplay distance should precede a production 4K default.

Dragons: current source and the matching target

Current src/creatures/wyvern/materials.ts generates body colors, countershading, scales, markings, scale relief and membrane detail in shaders from bind-space coordinates and uniforms. There are no body color/normal bitmap maps to resize. createBodyMaterial and createMembraneMaterial support shader detail variants; Wyvern currently constructs their default detail-2 variant. Wyvern.setQuality stores the quality level; it does not itself replace these materials with new bitmap maps. Resolution labels must not imply otherwise.

The table is the corresponding authoring/budget target if dragon bitmap materials are added in a separately scoped change. Retain the procedural breed patterns, recoloring, deformation, membrane backlight and distance filtering. Share reusable maps across compatible dragons; avoid allocating a full 4K set per individual. Measure body and wing texel density separately, and use close, chase-camera, bank/dive, day/night and multiple-dragon views to decide whether 4K adds useful detail. Do not convert dragons to PBR, migrate renderers or change their flight physics as a consequence of this budget. No dragon runtime source is changed by this work.

Cloth behavior in this proof

Loose Wanderer drape, sash, ponytail and selected existing accessories use the fixed-substep cloth solver with relative air, frame inertia, attachment tethers and body/mount capsules. C02 opts into non-bouncy contact and mild sliding friction; the ordinary wardrobe retains its existing contact arithmetic. Additional damping and a small low-speed flutter floor let loose linen react to a breeze and settle after gusts. Quality switches preserve simulation state.

Low particles can contact walkable ground/decks through the same groundAt query used by walking and foot placement. This is a walkable height surface, not arbitrary wall/foliage collision, wet-cloth simulation or self-collision. Ground projection is vertically resolved. Fitted chiton/trousers and Aerie's secured drape remain rig-driven; they do not flap like loose capes. The mounted costume, foot/stirrup fit, equipment sockets and flight simulation remain.
